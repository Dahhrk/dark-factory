# Product debate (default)

Default for every new product idea before implementation. Agents start
here automatically. Typing `/product-debate` is optional; the skill in
`cursor-team-kit` names the contract. Same shape as one-shot-task and
orwell-prose.

## Route

1. Open a temporary debate room whose name is plain product words.
   Not a bot name, not a role title, not Phase / Slice / Wave, not a
   standing channel.
2. Bring product, design, and engineering into that room (or the
   lane-native equivalent).
3. Capture requirements and the decision in writing.
4. Close the room after the decision is captured.
5. Only then start implementation under the one-shot route.

## Lane-native room

- Where group channels exist: open a temporary named group channel,
  capture the decision, then close or archive it.
- Other lanes: time-boxed discussion with a written decision. No fake
  `/loop`.

## Fail closed

Do not encode until the decision is captured in writing.

## Autopilot stays off

Leave Autopilot and TRUST-NEXT off unless Dark has already greened them
with evidence. Do not invent Autopilot. Do not self-merge.
