# Harness not training (default)

Ship harnesses and agents across factory lanes. Agents apply
automatically for capability / agent / bot / AI product work. Typing
`/harness-not-training` is optional; the skill in `cursor-team-kit`
names the contract. Same shape as leave-machine-clean.

## Route

1. Capability / agent / bot / AI product asks go to harness, agent,
   eval, or delivery path.
2. Cheap abundant delivery of existing model intelligence beats another
   training run for this factory.
3. Fine-tune, train, or LoRA only when Dark names that path. Otherwise
   fail closed and redirect to harness work.
4. Autopilot and TRUST-NEXT stay off unless already greened with
   evidence. Do not invent Autopilot. Do not self-merge.

## Fail closed

Proposing or starting frontier training without an explicit train ask
from Dark is not done. Redirect to harness work and say why.

## Autopilot stays off

Leave Autopilot and TRUST-NEXT off unless Dark has already greened them
with evidence. Do not invent Autopilot. Do not self-merge.
